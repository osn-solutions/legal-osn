**Operational & Strategic Networks Inc. Accessibility Statement**

Last updated: <mark>[EFFECTIVE DATE]</mark>, 2026

Operational & Strategic Networks Inc. ("**OSN**") wants osnplatform.com and SABRE to be usable by everyone, including people with disabilities.

## Our standard

We aim to conform to the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.1, Level AA, published by the World Wide Web Consortium (W3C). We have not yet completed a formal audit, so we do not claim full conformance today.

## Known limitations

- **Desktop only.** SABRE is built for laptop and desktop screens. It does not currently support phones or small tablets.
- **Maps and the game board.** Exercises run on an interactive map. Some map content, such as unit positions and terrain, is shown visually and may not be fully available to screen readers or keyboard-only users.
- **Third-party map content.** Some map imagery comes from third-party providers, and we cannot fully control its accessibility.

We are working on these. If one of them blocks you or someone in your Organization, contact us. We will try to work with your Organization to find another way to take part in an exercise.

## Feedback and help

If you have trouble using any part of osnplatform.com or SABRE, email leo@osnplatform.com. Tell us the page or feature and the problem. We aim to reply within <mark>[5]</mark> business days.

Organizations that need a formal accessibility report, such as a Voluntary Product Accessibility Template (VPAT), can request one. We will tell you when it is available.
