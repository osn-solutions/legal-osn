Some map imagery and base maps in SABRE come from the providers below. They do not receive Organization data. If a layer loads directly from a provider, your browser sends that provider your IP address and the map area requested. <mark>[CONFIRM WHICH OF THESE LOAD DIRECTLY IN THE BROWSER.]</mark>

| Provider | Content |
| --- | --- |
| Esri | World Imagery satellite base layer and land cover data |
