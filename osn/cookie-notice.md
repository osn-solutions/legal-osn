**What are cookies?**

Cookies are small data files that are placed on your computer or mobile device when you visit a website. Cookies serve different purposes, like keeping you signed in, protecting your session, letting you move between pages efficiently, and helping us understand how a site is being used.

Our Sites may use both session cookies (which expire once you close your web browser) and persistent cookies (which stay on your device until they expire or you delete them).

We use two broad categories of cookies: (1) first-party cookies, set by OSN; and (2) third-party cookies, set by our service providers on our Sites.

**Other technologies**

In addition to cookies, our Sites may use other technologies to collect information automatically.

*Browser Web Storage*

The SABRE application may use browser web storage (such as local storage and session storage) for similar purposes as cookies, for example to keep an exercise running smoothly. Browser web storage can hold more data than cookies. Your web browser may let you clear your browser web storage.

*Web Beacons*

We may use web beacons (also known as pixel tags or clear GIFs) in our HTML-formatted emails to learn whether emails were opened and links were clicked. This helps us manage our communications. Most email clients let you block images, which prevents web beacons from loading.

# Your choices

Your options for controlling what information cookies and similar technologies collect about you include:

1. **Blocking cookies in your browser.** Most browsers let you remove or reject cookies. To do this, follow the instructions in your browser settings. Many browsers accept cookies by default until you change your settings. If you disable cookies, the Sites may not work properly. For example, you will not be able to sign in to SABRE or load maps. For more information about cookies, including how to see what cookies have been set on your device and how to manage and delete them, visit [allaboutcookies.org](https://www.allaboutcookies.org).
2. **Blocking images and clear GIFs.** Most browsers and email clients let you prevent images from loading. To do this, follow the instructions in your browser, device, or email settings.
3. **Global Privacy Control.** We honor Global Privacy Control (GPC) browser signals as a request to opt out of any sale or sharing of personal information. We do not sell or share personal information for advertising in any case.
