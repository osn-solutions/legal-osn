## Facts used

- **Entity:** Operational & Strategic Networks Inc., Delaware corporation (incorporated April 8, 2026)
- **Address for notices:** 119 Berkeley Pl., Brooklyn, NY 11217
- **Contact for legal, privacy, and support:** leo@osnplatform.com
- **Governing law and venue:** Delaware; courts in New Castle County, Delaware
- **Arbitration:** JAMS, individual only, by video or in the user's county, 30-day opt-out. Does not apply to U.S. government users.
- **Hosting:** Amazon Web Services, United States. Cloudflare for content delivery and security.
- **Minimum age:** 13. Users under 18 only through an organization with any required parental consent.
- **Liability cap:** greater of $100 or fees paid in the prior 12 months
- **Retention:** 90 days after a subscription ends; usage logs up to 12 months
- **Data commitments:** no sale, no targeted ads, model training with an organization opt-out, no publishing data that identifies a unit, location, capability, or readiness level, no CUI or classified data

Sources: the SABRE Pilot Agreement template, the Research Deployment Agreement draft, the MI foreign qualification, and the OSN Legal & Compliance drafts in the OSN Legal folder.
